    - A Vigência search on those four lists that shows the assumed validity, version, and record day before the filter runs
    - A refusal when the day does not exist or when a validity date and a registered version are combined, and a notice when a period is reordered earlier-to-later

## Context

The classifier keeps validity time and record time. The four changelists can already narrow by record status, including the sentinel record end. They do not offer one question for a date, a period, a registered version, or the span between two registered versions, as known now or as known on a given day.

Issue `#87` records that question. The settled behavior, including the twenty-four worked cases and the four questions still open, is the conception archived with this plan. LDO and LOA-PPAG are prefixed dates, not rows of the version table.

`#34` is a public-dashboard point-in-time query. `#67` added category and level filters on the item list. Neither is this search.

## Objective

A person can ask which codes are valid for a chosen window, and the four lists can apply that same question. The window follows the conception. The four open questions in that file stay unanswered until the operator decides them.

The work stays on the existing branch `migracao`. This plan does not open another branch.

### mid-01 — One validity window

**Context:** The conception already fixes how a partial date, a period, a prefixed mark, and a record day become a window. Cases 16 and 22, and the other questions in section 10, are not fixed.
**Objective:** Every caller can obtain that window for the settled cases, and can be told when the request is refused or reordered.
**Issue(s):** `—`
**Scope:**
  `In scope:` Completion of empty year, month, and day. A point versus a period that the code must cover entirely. Sentinel record end, or an as-of record day. LDO on 15 May, including when another record day was typed. Reorder of an inverted period, with a notice. Refusal of an impossible day and of a validity date combined with a registered version.
  `Out of scope:` Choosing the calendar day for LOA-PPAG. Choosing how a registered version becomes a date span. Choosing one version control or two. Treating the `2025` in case 16 as settled.
**Acceptance criteria:**
  - [ ] Cases 1 to 15, 17 to 21, 23, and 24 of the conception produce the assumed validity, version, record day, and filter written there.
  - [ ] Case 11 does not search, and the message names the day field.
  - [ ] Case 15 searches the reordered window and states the reorder.
  - [ ] Case 24 does not search, and the message says a validity date and a registered version were combined.
  - [ ] Section 10 of the conception is still unanswered.

### mid-02 — Inquiry outside the lists

**Context:** The same question is needed when the person is not standing on a changelist.
**Objective:** An inquiry accepts dates or registered versions, and an optional as-of record day, and returns the window from mid-01.
**Issue(s):** `—`
**Scope:**
  `In scope:` Dates, or one registered version, or two registered versions. LDO and LOA-PPAG travel with the year as prefixed dates. A request that carries both a validity date and a registered version is refused.
  `Out of scope:` A second meaning of the window. Answering section 10.
**Acceptance criteria:**
  - [ ] The inquiry and mid-01 agree on every settled case.
  - [ ] A registered version leaves the validity dates unused.
  - [ ] An empty record day keeps the sentinel record end, except when LDO or LOA-PPAG sets that day.

### mid-03 — Vigência search on the four lists

**Context:** Series, classifications, hierarchical levels, and classification items are the lists named in the conception. The existing record-status filter stays.
**Objective:** Each of those lists can run the mid-01 window from a Vigência search, and the person sees the assumed window.
**Issue(s):** `—`
**Scope:**
  `In scope:` Validity date, version, and record date on each of the four lists. A search action applies the window. Registered active versions are offered, with LDO and LOA-PPAG fixed at the top of the version control. The search runs when the person asks, including when the fields are empty.
  `Out of scope:` Abbreviation lists, legal bases, variants, and the version list as targets. Replacing the record-status filter. Creating, editing, or closing a code. Changing what the list shows before the person searches.
**Acceptance criteria:**
  - [ ] The same inputs yield the same window on each of the four lists and from the inquiry.
  - [ ] The assumed validity, version, and record day are visible when the search runs.
  - [ ] A reorder still searches. A refusal does not.
  - [ ] The record-status filter still works when this search is not used.
